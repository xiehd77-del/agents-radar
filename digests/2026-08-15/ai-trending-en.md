## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

**Agent infrastructure is exploding.** The most striking pattern in today's data is that agent *infrastructure* — not models, not generic AI tools — is capturing the community's attention. ego-lite (a browser for agents), holaOS (agent workspaces), and macro (agent-shared memory) all represent a second wave of agent tooling: moving beyond the "agent harness" era into **workspaces, browsers, and memory layers** purpose-built for AI agents. The community is no longer asking "how do I build an agent?" but "how do I give my agents a home?"

**Edge AI is becoming real.** The needle project's 14MB foundation model represents a dramatic step toward true edge deployment — not just quantized models, but *architecturally tiny* models designed for phones, wearables, and robots. Combined with picollm's X-bit quantization, the trend toward **sub-20MB on-device intelligence** is emerging as a distinct category.

**The spec-driven development movement is mainstreaming.** GitHub's own spec-kit surging today (1,160 stars) suggests that **spec-driven development** — declaring what you want, then having AI implement it — is becoming standardized practice, not just a niche technique.

**Local-first AI is consolidating.** Unsloth's expansion from fine-tuning into a full local UI for running and training both LLMs and diffusion models signals that **the fine-tuning layer is merging with the inference/runtime layer** — one tool to run and train everything locally.

**RAG is evolving beyond vectors.** PageIndex's "vectorless, reasoning-based RAG" and semantica's graph-native approach both point to a **"post-vector" RAG direction** — knowledge graphs and reasoning-based retrieval replacing pure embedding similarity. This is a significant architectural shift.
