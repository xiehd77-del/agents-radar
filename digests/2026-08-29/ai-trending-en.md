## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

The dominant signal today is the **commoditization of agent skills and plugins**. With Anthropic launching an official plugin directory and Cursor releasing its plugin spec, the ecosystem is consolidating around a "skill-as-package" model—turning agent capabilities into installable, shareable artifacts. This is the strongest "platform moment" for AI coding agents yet.

**Token/efficiency optimization** has emerged as a full category: headroom (60–95% compression), caveman (65% token reduction via simplified language), and ponytail (lazy-senior-dev behavioral shaping) all attack the same cost bottleneck from different angles. This indicates the community is moving from "can agents do it?" to "can agents do it cheaply at scale?"

**Knowledge graphs are making a comeback** as the preferred RAG architecture. GitNexus and Graphify both reject vector stores in favor of deterministic AST-based knowledge graphs for code, suggesting a backlash against probabilistic retrieval for structured domains. This pairs with mem0's memory layer—agents need structured, persistent understanding, not just similarity search.

**Agentic RL** (AgentsMeetRL) appears as a new research direction linking reinforcement learning to agent behavior improvement—a tip of the iceberg for "training agents to be better agents" rather than just prompting them.

The concentration of six-figure star counts on agent-related repos (ECC at 244k, hermes-agent at 238k) versus infrastructure (transformers at 165k) suggests the community's center of gravity has shifted decisively from model-building to agent-application.
