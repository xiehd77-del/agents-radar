**A new architectural pattern is emerging: "vectorless RAG."** VectifyAI's PageIndex (34K stars) and StarTrail's LEANN (97% fewer storage bytes) challenge the vector-embedding orthodoxy — suggesting **reasoning-based retrieval can beat brute-force similarity search** for many use cases.
